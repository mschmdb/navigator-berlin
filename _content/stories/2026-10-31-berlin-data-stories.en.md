Atlas and Kiez Finder show Berlin as a map: noise, heat, green space, elections, for every address. That's handy. Still, something was missing for me. Much of the data has more to tell than a map can show.

That's what the Data Stories are for. Here I work with the data until it turns into a picture or a story. Sometimes that's a poster for your wall, sometimes a longer interactive story. Not all of it would pass a data visualisation class (if you've got Tufte on your shelf: I know). The exact numbers are still in the atlas.

First up is "Point and Line to Kiez": [the twelve districts as compositions of shapes](/en/stories/punkt-linie-kiez-bezirke). More will follow, picture by picture, story by story. First the districts, then the Kieze, with single topics in between.

## Ready to print

Many pictures come as PDFs. Want your district on the wall? Download the file and have it printed, at any size.
