## Analytics

Our own [Plausible](https://plausible.io) instance runs on a second German server. It is open source, uses no cookies, stores no IP addresses long term and respects "Do Not Track". No cookie banner is needed.

Each page view records the URL, country or region, browser and device type. The data is aggregated, without identifiers.

Five additional events:

- **Search**: an address was searched for and selected.
- **Bookmark**: an address was saved. It is stored locally in the browser.
- **Compare**: compare mode was activated.
- **Share**: the share sheet was opened.
- **Locate**: the location button on the map was used.

No tracking cookies, no advertising cookies, no browser fingerprints, no cross-site linking, no sharing with third parties.

Details: [Privacy](/en/datenschutz).
