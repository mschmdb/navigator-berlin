The pages for the 143 Kieze and 12 districts used to be long tables that repeated each other. Now they tell the place by topic and let you compare. One example is [Reuterstraße](/en/kiez/reuterstrasse).

## Chapters instead of tables

Every Kiez page is split into chapters: noise and air, green and heat, housing and rent, getting around, shops, daycare and culture, people, elections and neighbours. At the end you find the [Kiez score](/en/kiez-score) and frequently asked questions.

- **Charts.** Every value appears among all 143 Kieze or all 12 districts. A short sentence next to it tells you how to read it.
- **Charts or tables.** A switch shows the same values as a table.
- **Per 10,000 residents.** Counts such as daycare centres or stops are shown per 10,000 residents, so large and small Kieze compare fairly.

## Compare

A switch at the top of the page sets what you compare with: Berlin, the own district, a neighbouring Kiez, a similar Kiez or any Kiez. Charts, sentences and tables follow your choice. The comparison is part of the address, so you can share it.

The [district pages](/en/bezirk/friedrichshain-kreuzberg) show the district among all 12 and the spread of its own Kieze.

## Also fixed

- **Ranks with ties.** Equal values now share a rank, as in a competition.
- **Frequently asked questions.** We checked every answer against the data and rewrote it: the verdict first, then the number, source and date at the end.
- **Green supply.** It now uses the Senate's terms: good, medium, poor.
